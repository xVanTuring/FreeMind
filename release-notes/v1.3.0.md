## FreeMind 1.3.0

### 缩略图
- 画布右下角显示整张导图的缩略图和当前可视范围，点击或拖动即可跳到导图的任意位置
- 不需要时可以关掉：“显示 ▸ 显示缩略图”（⌥⌘M）

### Finder 空格预览
- 在 Finder 里对 .fmind 文件按空格预览时，可以按住拖动查看导图的各个部分
- 捏合或按住 ⌘ 滚动鼠标滚轮可以缩放
- 打开时整张图缩放到窗口放得下的大小；导图太大时不会缩到字看不清，而是以中心主题为准显示一部分

## FreeMind 1.3.0

### Minimap
- A minimap in the bottom-right corner of the canvas shows the whole map and the visible area; click or drag in it to jump anywhere in the map
- Turn it off when you don't need it: View ▸ Show Minimap (⌥⌘M)

### Finder preview
- When you press Space on a .fmind file in Finder, you can drag the preview to look around the map
- Pinch, or hold ⌘ and use the mouse wheel, to zoom
- The preview opens with the whole map fitted to the window; very large maps open around the central topic instead of being shrunk until the text is unreadable
