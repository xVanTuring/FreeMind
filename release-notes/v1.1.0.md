## FreeMind 1.1.0

### 联系线
- 按住联系线的线条或标签直接拖动就能调整弧度，不用先选中再找控制柄
- 标签自动错开：几条联系线的标签不再叠在一起，也不会被别的线压住
- 联系线太多觉得干扰时可以全部隐藏：“显示 ▸ 显示联系线”（⌥⌘L），或右键联系线选“隐藏所有联系线”。隐藏状态随文件保存，状态栏会提示隐藏了几条，点一下恢复显示

### 其他改进
- 触控板双指滚动可以往任意方向拖动画布，不再只能水平或垂直
- 再次打开 FreeMind 时会切换到已经运行的那个，不会同时开出两个

### 问题修复
- 备注、超链接、标签等弹出框的内容显示不全

## FreeMind 1.1.0

### Relationships
- Drag a relationship line or its label to reshape it; no need to select it and find the handles first
- Labels move apart automatically, so they no longer pile up on top of each other or get crossed by other lines
- Hide all relationships when they get in the way: View ▸ Show Relationships (⌥⌘L), or right-click a relationship and choose Hide All Relationships. The setting is saved with the file, and the status bar shows how many are hidden; click it to show them again

### Other improvements
- Two-finger scrolling on the trackpad moves the map in any direction instead of only horizontally or vertically
- Opening FreeMind again switches to the copy that is already running instead of starting a second one

### Fixes
- Notes, hyperlink, label and other popovers were cut off at the edges
