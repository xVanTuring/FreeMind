## FreeMind 1.4.0

### 新结构：流程串
- 各阶段按顺序从左往右排在一条线上，用箭头串联；每个阶段的细节在它下面向下展开，适合整理流程、步骤和知识串
- 在工具栏的“结构”、菜单“修改 ▸ 结构”或右侧格式面板里切换，已有的导图切过去不用重做
- ← / → 在阶段之间移动，↓ 进入这个阶段的细节；⌥↑ / ⌥↓ 调整阶段的先后，箭头跟着重新串联
- 模板库新增“流程”模板
- 导入 XMind 的水平时间轴时使用流程串结构

### 问题修复
- 画布右下角的缩略图在 Retina 屏幕上只画出一半大小，和可视范围框对不上

## FreeMind 1.4.0

### New structure: process chain
- Stages are laid out from left to right on one line and joined by arrows in order; the details of each stage are listed below it. Good for processes, steps and study outlines
- Switch from Structure in the toolbar, Modify ▸ Structure, or the format panel; existing maps switch over without any rework
- ← / → move between stages and ↓ goes into the details of a stage; ⌥↑ / ⌥↓ change the order of stages and the arrows follow
- New “Process” template in the template gallery
- XMind maps with a horizontal timeline are imported as a process chain

### Fixes
- On Retina displays the minimap in the bottom-right corner was drawn at half size and did not line up with the visible-area frame
